Relocating FAQ

Can you handle the entire sale remotely?

Yes. We use video walkthroughs, electronic signatures, and mobile notaries when needed. Many of our relocating clients never return to Kansas City during the selling process.

How quickly can you close for a job transfer?

Cash offers can close in as little as two weeks. We coordinate with your employer's relocation timeline and can adjust dates to match your start date.

What about employer relocation packages?

We provide documentation employers typically require for relocation benefits. Many corporate packages include cash offer bonuses or closing cost assistance that we can help coordinate.

Can you work with my employer's relocation package?

Yes. We provide documentation employers typically require and can coordinate with relocation companies. We're experienced with corporate transfer processes.

Do I need to move everything out first?

No. We coordinate clean-out around your moving schedule. Many relocating sellers leave items behind, and we handle removal as part of our offer.

What if my new home purchase depends on this sale?

We structure offers and timelines to coordinate with your new home closing. Many relocating sellers use our cash offers to make stronger offers on their next home.

What if the house needs repairs?

We buy relocating properties as-is. No need to coordinate contractors from a distance. We handle all repairs after closing. Visit our <a href="/solutions/vacant-house/" class="text-secondary underline">vacant property guide</a> for common issues.
